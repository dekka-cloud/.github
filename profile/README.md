### A cloud that's close to home, starting with Ghana

Dekka runs apps, databases and servers in Accra for the people building for Ghana, so your customers' data stays in the country and their requests don't have to travel to Europe and back. We share what we make along the way, and you're welcome to use it.

- [Veil](https://github.com/dekka-cloud/veil) finds and protects personal information in Ghanaian text, from Ghana Card PINs and mobile money numbers to names in Twi and Ewe. Install it from [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/dekka-veil/) with `pip install dekka-veil` or from [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/dekka-veil) with `npm install dekka-veil`.
- Veil's models, [veil-ner-nano](https://huggingface.co/dekka-cloud/veil-ner-nano) and [veil-ner-deep](https://huggingface.co/dekka-cloud/veil-ner-deep), and [veil-synth-gh](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dekka-cloud/veil-synth-gh), our synthetic dataset of Ghanaian text, are on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/dekka-cloud).
- The [Dekka CLI](https://github.com/dekka-cloud/dekka-cli) deploys and runs your apps from the terminal and from CI, with `npx dekka@latest login` to start, and it's on [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/dekka) too.
- [latency-bench](https://github.com/dekka-cloud/latency-bench) measures what distance costs the people using an app, and it holds the open data from our study from Accra.

Our [research](https://dekka.cloud/research) is where we publish what we learn about building for Ghana, such as [what personal data looks like here](https://dekka.cloud/research/personal-data-in-ghana) and [the round trip from Accra](https://dekka.cloud/research/round-trip-from-accra), always with the methods and data behind it.

You'll find the rest at [dekka.cloud](https://dekka.cloud) and in [the docs](https://docs.dekka.cloud), and you can follow along on [X](https://x.com/dekka_cloud) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dekka-cloud). If you're building something for people in Ghana, we'd love to hear about it, so [tell us what you're making](https://dekka.cloud/apply) or write to [hello@dekka.cloud](mailto:hello@dekka.cloud).
